## Known limitation

The file-store backend (`src/lib/store.ts`) keeps data in `/tmp` on Vercel,
which is per-instance and ephemeral — records can vanish between instances
(this proof survived because it was snapshotted, not because the store kept
it). For judging durability the backend should move to Postgres (Supabase) —
first item on the roadmap.
